About SB & Associates

Three decades of legal excellence serving Delhi and beyond

Our Journey

Founded with a vision to provide comprehensive legal solutions, SB & Associates has grown from a small practice to one of Delhi's most respected law firms. Our journey began in the early 1990s when Mr. Shailendra Bhatnagar established the firm with a simple yet powerful philosophy: deliver practical, result-oriented legal advice that makes a real difference in our clients' lives.

Over three decades, we have built a reputation for excellence in handling complex legal matters across multiple jurisdictions. Our evolution from a traditional practice to a modern, business-focused law firm reflects our commitment to adapting with the changing legal landscape while maintaining the highest standards of professional integrity.

1993 Established practice, Bar Council of Delhi membership
2000s Expanded to corporate and banking law
2010s Supreme Court practice established
Present DIAC Arbitrator, leading Delhi practice

Leadership

Meet the founder and driving force behind SB & Associates

Mr. Shailendra Bhatnagar

Senior Advocate & Founder

Mr. Shailendra Bhatnagar is a distinguished advocate whose career spans over three decades of exceptional legal practice. A graduate of Delhi University (1986), he has established himself as one of Delhi's most respected legal practitioners, known for his strategic approach and unwavering commitment to client success.

His practice encompasses a broad spectrum of legal disciplines, from high-stakes commercial litigation to complex regulatory matters. Mr. Bhatnagar's expertise is particularly renowned in the areas of banking law, debt recovery, and corporate advisory services, where his practical approach has helped numerous clients navigate challenging legal landscapes.

As a regular practitioner before the Supreme Court of India, High Courts, and various regulatory authorities, Mr. Bhatnagar brings a unique perspective that combines appellate court experience with ground-level commercial understanding. His appointment as an arbitrator with the Delhi International Arbitration Centre (DIAC) and his role as a trained mediator with the Delhi High Court further underscore his standing in the legal community.

Professional Credentials

  • Member, Bar Council of Delhi (since 1993)
