About SB & Associates

Full-service Indian law firm delivering comprehensive legal solutions

SB & Associates is a full-service Indian law firm delivering comprehensive legal solutions across corporate, civil, criminal, regulatory, and advisory matters. With over three decades of legal practice, the firm combines litigation strength with business focused advisory, serving a diverse clientele that includes corporates, financial institutions, promoter groups, and individuals.

Led by Mr. Shailendra Bhatnagar, an advocate with more than 30 years of experience, SB & Associates represents clients before the Supreme Court, High Courts, District Courts and regulatory authorities nationwide.

Our people are our strength, a team that balances legal precision with pragmatic advice. The firm is recognized for its practical, result-oriented approach and its commitment to safeguarding client interests.
