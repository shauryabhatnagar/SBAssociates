About Our Firm

SB & Associates is a full-service Indian law firm with over 30 years of experience. We practice before the Supreme Court of India, various High Courts, and District Courts across the country, combining strong litigation skills with practical, business-focused advisory. Our areas of practice span civil and criminal matters, corporate and commercial law, alternative dispute resolution, consumer disputes, matrimonial and family matters, property and real estate issues, and banking and finance including SARFAESI proceedings. Serving corporates, financial institutions, and individuals across Delhi NCR and beyond, we are committed to providing clear, effective, and dependable legal solutions tailored to every client's needs.
