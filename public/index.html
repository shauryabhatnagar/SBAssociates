About Us

SHAILENDER BHATNAGAR & ASSOCIATES (SBA) has a diversified legal practice with an equally strong corporate, civil and criminal practice. In addition to specialized areas of practice, the associates bring to the firm a diverse background with experience in litigation, corporate practices and corporate in-house counselling. This wealth of knowledge enables SBA to offer our clients comprehensive solutions to their most challenging legal issues within the clients’ deadlines in virtually every area of law.

Our Domain

Practice Areas

Our attorneys are able to draw on their unique expertise in both civil and criminal law and collaborate to ensure that both areas are considered in all transaction, litigation and general advisory services rendered to our clients. This approach, however, is undertaken in the most pragmatic fashion in order to provide our clients with comprehensive but economically efficient advice.

As a matter of fact, we at SBA jokingly refer to our advice as ‘pragmalegal’ advice.

Litigation

With over 25 years of experience in handling a variety of commercial and business disputes, we have a successful litigation practice by developing and devising the best possible effective strategies for our Clients. Thus, we have learned to use litigation as an effective tool to meet our Client’s needs – turning the negative aspect of Indian Court Delays into an advantage for our clients and provide our litigation expertise across the country.

Sarfaesi

We specialize in this activity in obtaining short dates and secured order from CMMs & DMs. We also specialize in networking with Police and concerned authorities qua to Sarfaesi Act (Securitization and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act).

Consumer Disputes

We can defend our client in any kind of consumer disputes if take place in all the consumer forums i.e. National, State and district.

In any kind of unnecessary grievance that takes place to spoil your reputation we shall give our best to keep up the dignity of such a big organization as yours.

Bankruptcy

We have a team devoted to Financial Resolution, Bankruptcy and Reorganization which specializes in credit risk control, fraud management and debt resolution.

We are also well versed and involved in the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code process.(NCLT).

Legal Audit

It being one of the most important aspects of our profession, we would review bills to ensure compliance with customary and reasonable billing standards and guidelines. We would guide you with simple and sorted ways, which shall make your transactions clear and lawful.

Arbitration

In case of Arbitration clause in the agreement we undertake the entire arbitral proceedings up to Award stage. In case of any challenge of Award we do all the legal proceedings. We do specialize in various sections of Arbitration Conciliation Act.

Contracts and Agreements

We can prepare, advice and assist on any kind of contracts or agreements being entered upon by our client, we make sure that every clause in the contract never causes grievance to our client.
