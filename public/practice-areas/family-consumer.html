Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on family, matrimonial, and consumer law matters

How do I file a consumer complaint?

A written complaint is filed before the District, State, or National Commission depending on the value of the claim, generally within two years of the cause of action. E-filing is available, and relief can include refund, replacement, compensation, and litigation costs.

What counts as deficiency of service?

Any shortfall in the quality or manner of a service compared to what was promised or reasonably expected — delayed possession by a builder, wrongly repudiated insurance claims, defective goods, or negligent professional services.

What is the process for divorce by mutual consent?

The spouses jointly petition after settling terms on maintenance, custody, and property. The law provides for two motions separated by a cooling-off period, which courts may waive in appropriate cases. It is usually the fastest and least adversarial route.

How is maintenance decided?

Courts weigh the income and earning capacity of both spouses, the standard of living during the marriage, and the needs of children. Maintenance can be interim (during proceedings) as well as permanent.
