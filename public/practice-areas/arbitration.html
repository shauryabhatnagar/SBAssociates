Expert ADR Services

Our ADR practice emphasizes resolving disputes outside the courts through arbitration, mediation, and conciliation. We at SB & Associates represent clients before arbitral tribunals in India and assist in drafting agreements and enforcing awards.

Along with arbitration, we guide parties in mediation and conciliation to achieve amicable, cost-effective, and time-efficient settlements. These methods provide practical and confidential alternatives to lengthy court proceedings while protecting our clients' interests.

Adv. Shailendra Bhatnagar is a trained Mediator in the Delhi High Court and also a member of DIAC (Delhi International Arbitration Center) as an Arbitrator appointed by the Delhi High Court, bringing unique dual perspective to dispute resolution.

Why Choose ADR Over Litigation

Benefits of alternative dispute resolution methods

Time Efficiency

ADR processes typically resolve disputes in 6-12 months compared to 3-5 years in traditional litigation

Confidentiality

All ADR proceedings are private and confidential, protecting business reputation and sensitive information

Cost Effective

Significantly lower costs compared to court litigation, with predictable fee structures and shorter timelines

Relationship Preservation

Collaborative approach helps maintain business relationships and enables future cooperation between parties

Flexibility & Control

Parties have greater control over the process, timeline, and choice of arbitrator or mediator

International Enforceability

Arbitral awards are easily enforceable across 160+ countries under New York Convention
